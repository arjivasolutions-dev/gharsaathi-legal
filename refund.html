Refund & Cancellation Policy

App: GharSaathi (com.arjiva.gharsathi)

1. In-App Subscriptions & Purchases

All in-app purchases and GharSaathi Plus subscriptions are processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing. As transactions are managed directly by Google, refund requests are primarily subject to Google Play Refund Policies.

2. How to Cancel a Subscription

You can cancel your subscription at any time without penalty:

  • Open Google Play Store on your Android device.
  • Tap your profile icon > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  • Select GharSaathi and tap Cancel Subscription.

3. Direct Developer Assistance

If you experienced an accidental duplicate charge or technical glitch where premium features were not unlocked despite payment, email us at arjivasolutions@gmail.com with your Google Play Order Transaction ID (starts with GPA.xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxx). Valid claims will be reviewed and refunded within 5-7 business days.
