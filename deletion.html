Account & Data Deletion Request

At GharSaathi, we prioritize your data privacy. You can completely erase your account, paired family associations, reminders, and synced records at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion
Open GharSaathi > Go to Settings > Select Account & Security > Tap Delete Account & Data. All stored records will be wiped immediately.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request (If app is uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to arjivasolutions@gmail.com from your registered account email.
  2. Use the subject line: [Data Deletion] GharSaathi Account - Your Name.
  3. State clearly that you request the deletion of your account and associated database records.

Retention Period: Upon verification, all your cloud database entries (voice transcripts, shared lists, user pairing records) will be permanently purged within 30 days. No lingering backup copies will be kept.
