Privacy Policy for GharSaathi (घर साथी)

Effective Date: October 14, 2026  |  App: GharSaathi: Smart Household Diary

At GharSaathi (developed by Arjiva Solutions), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy details our data collection, processing, storage, and security practices in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global data privacy standards.

1. Data We Collect and How We Collect It

We only collect information necessary to deliver voice-first productivity, expense management, and household coordination features:

  • Account Details: Your name, email address, and profile picture when you authenticate via Google Sign-In or other supported sign-in options.
  • User Inputs & Household Data: Reminders, to-do tasks, expense logs, grocery pantry items, notes, and memory entries created manually or transcribed via voice.
  • Family & Delegation Data: Unique household pairing keys, user IDs, and task assignment records shared between linked household members.
  • Document Metatags: Document titles and category records stored in your personal document checklist (we do not access your confidential documents unless explicitly attached by you).
  • App Performance & Diagnostics: Crash logs, error diagnostics, and basic device metrics to optimize offline performance and fix bugs.

2. Audio and Microphone Permissions (Voice-First Engine)

Microphone Usage: GharSaathi accesses the device microphone strictly upon user tap to capture voice commands (e.g., setting a reminder or adding a grocery item).

  • On-Device Offline Processing: For core commands, voice-to-text processing occurs locally on your device whenever supported by your operating system, ensuring zero audio upload.
  • No Continuous Listening: GharSaathi does not record ambient background sound. The microphone is only active while the voice prompt popup is visible and listening.
  • Audio Retention: Raw audio recordings are neither permanently stored on our servers nor sold to third parties. Only the resulting text transcripts (e.g., "Buy 2 kg flour") are saved locally or synchronized to your account.

3. Permissions Breakdown

Permission Purpose Necessity

INTERNET / NETWORK Authenticates accounts, synchronizes household pairing, and enables optional cloud backups. Required for Cloud Sync

4. Cloud Synchronization and Family Sharing

GharSaathi offers robust offline functionality. However, if you choose to enable cloud backup or family sharing:

  • Data is synchronized securely via encrypted industry-standard cloud protocols (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Family Pairing: Only paired family members who share a mutual primary authorization key can access delegated tasks or collaborative grocery lists.
  • WhatsApp Sharing: When sharing grocery lists via WhatsApp, data is transferred directly using Android's native sharing intents. We do not inspect or store your external messaging contacts.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We may partner with reputable third-party cloud infrastructure and analytics providers to ensure uptime and service stability:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: For account authentication, real-time database sync, and crash reporting.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: To securely process transactions for GharSaathi Plus / VIP subscriptions without storing financial credentials on our servers.

6. Data Retention and Security

We implement administrative, organizational, and technical security protocols to safeguard your personal household data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Data stored locally on your device remains until you clear the app data or uninstall the application. Cloud-stored data is retained as long as your account remains active.

7. Account and Complete Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion requirement, you have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: You can initiate account deletion directly from the App Settings > Profile > Delete Account.
  • Manual Web Request: You can email us at arjivaapps@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - GharSaathi" using your registered account email. All corresponding cloud records, paired family links, and sync entries will be permanently removed within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

GharSaathi is intended for general household and family task management. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new app features or updated regulatory standards. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Effective Date". Continued use of GharSaathi following any modification signifies acceptance of the revised policy.
